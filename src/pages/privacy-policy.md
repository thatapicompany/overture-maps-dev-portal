# Privacy Policy

:::info Summary at a glance
- We collect minimal information to run the API.
- We log each API request with the account that made it, including its search filters. Coordinates are rounded to about 1 km before they are stored.
- We use those records for billing, support, abuse prevention and improving the service, not to identify or track your end users.
- Infrastructure and hosting use GCP, Cloudflare, and Vercel.
- Server request logs are kept for 30 days and usage records for 400 days.
- Google Analytics helps us understand which parts of the site and docs need improvement.
- You can request access or deletion of your data.
- We keep the service secure.
:::

## Who we are
- We operate the Overture Maps API.
- We design it with privacy first.

## Information we collect
- Account details: your email address, name, organisation and plan.
- A usage record for each API request: the account and a hash of the key that made it, the time, the endpoint, the search filters sent (such as categories, brand names, country and radius), the response status and size, and the client's User-Agent and Origin headers.
- Coordinates in a request (`lat`, `lng`, `bbox`) are rounded to two decimal places, about 1 km, before the usage record is stored.
- Server request logs from our hosting provider, which hold the full request address, including exact coordinates, and the caller's IP address.
- We never store your API key itself in usage records, only a hash.

## How we use information
- To provide the API.
- To debug and improve the service.
- Google Analytics on the main website to understand which sections need improvement.
- A first-party cookie (`om_first`) on the website remembers how you first found us: the referring site's host name, any campaign tags, the first page you opened and the date. If you later create an account, we store that source with the account so we can see which channels lead to sign-ups. It holds nothing else about you and lasts 90 days.
- To bill correctly, apply plan limits, answer support questions and prevent abuse.
- To email you about your account, such as when you are close to a limit or a request keeps failing.
- To see, in aggregate, which places, brands and categories people look up, so we know where to improve the data.
- We do not use request data to identify or track your end users, and we do not sell it.

## Data retention
- Server request logs are deleted after 30 days.
- Usage records are deleted after 400 days.
- Account details are kept while your account is open.

## Data sharing
- We do not sell personal data.
- We share data only with service providers.
- GCP for infrastructure and hosting.
- Cloudflare for content delivery and security.
- Vercel for website deployment.
- Stripe for payments, Postmark for email and Twenty for customer records.
- Google Analytics on the website.
- They process data only to provide their service to us.

## Your rights
- You may request a copy of your data.
- You may ask us to delete your data.
- Contact us at aden@thatapicompany.com

## Changes
- We may update this policy.
- We will post any changes on this page.

This Privacy Policy is provided for general information and is not legal advice.
